What you are getting

ShopWithEzz helps you create shopping lists, add items by voice, barcode scanning or photos, track prices and totals, set a budget and keep your spending under control.

It also includes sharing, recipes, backup and restore, and help information.

Yearly access

All features are available for 31 days after installation. The one-year full-access purchase can be made at any time during those 31 days in Settings for A$35, about 10 cents per day.

The purchase includes app updates during that paid year. There is no automatic renewal.

Android download

ShopWithEzz is currently available for Android through Google Play.
