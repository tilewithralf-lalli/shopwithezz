About ShopWithEzz

ShopWithEzz helps you create shopping lists, add items by typing, voice, barcode scanning or photos, track prices and watch your spending while you shop.

It is designed to make shopping less stressful and help you stay closer to your budget.

What You Can Do

  • Create and manage shopping lists
  • Add items by voice, barcode or photo
  • Track quantities, prices and totals
  • Set budgets and receive budget alerts
  • Use help and walkthrough information

One Simple Yearly Purchase

ShopWithEzz is a yearly purchase. All features are available for 31 days after installation.

The one-year full-access purchase can be made at any time during those 31 days in Settings for:

AUD $35

About 10 cents per day.

The purchase includes app updates during that paid year. Access continues until the paid year ends, and another year can be purchased whenever needed.

There is no automatic renewal.
