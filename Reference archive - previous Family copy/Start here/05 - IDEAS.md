# 05 - SHOPWITHEZZ IDEAS

## Purpose

This document is the parking place for possible future ShopWithEzz improvements.

An idea written here is **not approved work**, is **not part of the current release**, and must not be added to the app without clear approval.

The finished release stays protected while useful thoughts are saved for later.

---

## Idea Rules

Every future idea must:

1. Support the ShopWithEzz Bible.
2. Solve a real shopping problem.
3. Keep the app simple and uncluttered.
4. Pass the Granny Test.
5. Be quick, smart, fast, and reliable.
6. Protect privacy and user data.
7. Work offline where appropriate.
8. Be designed, built, tested, and approved one step at a time.

If an idea adds confusion without enough benefit, it should not be built.

---

## Status Key

- **Idea Only** - Saved for later discussion; not approved.
- **Review Later** - Worth investigating after the current release.
- **Approved for Future** - Explicitly approved but not started.
- **In Development** - Approved work has begun from a protected copy.
- **Completed** - Built, tested, approved, documented, and backed up.
- **Rejected** - Considered but deliberately not being built.

---

## Current Release Boundary

The current release is focused on:

- Simple shopping-list management.
- Clear budget tracking.
- Scanner and import tools.
- Hands-Free shopping commands.
- Sharing and printing.
- Settings, feedback, backup, restore, and About information.
- The Team Lalli61 31-day beta trial and one-time unlock system.

Optional future ideas must not delay the release unless they resolve a serious safety, privacy, reliability, or release-compliance problem.

---

## Future Idea Parking Area

### iPhone and iPad Version

**Status:** Review Later

Prepare and fully test an iOS version after the Android release is stable and approved.

Important: The iOS version must provide the same simple core experience, while clearly explaining any platform feature that works differently.

---

### Accessibility Review

**Status:** Review Later

Perform a dedicated accessibility review covering:

- Screen-reader labels.
- Text scaling.
- Colour contrast.
- Large tap targets.
- Voice-only use.
- Reduced-motion preferences where relevant.

This review must preserve the approved clean layout.

---

### More Natural Voice Phrases

**Status:** Idea Only

Consider additional everyday phrase variations only when real users repeatedly ask for them.

Any new phrase must be documented in How to Use and must not conflict with an existing command.

---

### Optional Aisle Ordering

**Status:** Idea Only

Consider allowing shoppers to arrange categories in the order they walk through their usual store.

This must remain optional and must not complicate the default Shopping List.

---

### Frequently Bought Items

**Status:** Idea Only

Consider a simple way to reuse common items without turning the home screen into a crowded catalogue.

The user must remain in control of what is saved and reused.

---

### Additional Languages

**Status:** Idea Only

Consider additional written and spoken languages after the English experience is stable.

Every translated command and message must be checked by a fluent speaker and tested on a real device.

---

## New Idea Template

Copy this section when recording a new idea:

### Idea Name

**Status:** Idea Only

**Problem it solves:**  
Describe the real user problem.

**Simple proposed solution:**  
Describe the smallest useful version.

**Who it helps:**  
Describe the intended user.

**Granny Test:**  
Explain why a first-time user would understand it.

**Privacy or data impact:**  
Explain what information is used, stored, shared, or deleted.

**Offline impact:**  
Explain whether it works without internet access.

**Testing required:**  
List the real-device checks needed before approval.

**Decision:**  
Idea Only / Review Later / Approved for Future / Rejected

---

## Approval Gate

Before any idea moves into development:

1. Confirm the current release is protected by a verified backup.
2. Confirm the idea solves a real problem.
3. Agree on the smallest useful version.
4. Identify the files and features it may affect.
5. Decide how it will be tested.
6. Receive clear approval to begin.

Development then starts from a safe copy and follows the standard Team Lalli61 workflow.
