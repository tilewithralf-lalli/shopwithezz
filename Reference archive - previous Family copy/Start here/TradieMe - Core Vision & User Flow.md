# TradieMe - Core Vision & User Flow

## Purpose

TradieMe is **not just another tradie directory**.

Its purpose is to help people quickly understand:

1.  Who they need.
2.  What it should cost.
3.  Who they can contact.

The app should be faster and easier than searching on Google.

# Core Principles

-   Keep the experience simple.
-   Pass the Granny Test.
-   Never overwhelm the user.
-   The journey should take less than 10 seconds whenever possible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Important Rules

## Information First

Every user wants to know:

1.  Who do I need?
2.  What should it cost?
3.  Who can I call?

Always answer those questions in that order.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Don't Diagnose

TradieMe does not diagnose problems.

It simply suggests the services that should help based on the user's
description.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Search Experience

-   Search stays visible.
-   Keyboard closes after searching.
-   Results appear immediately underneath.
-   Keep navigation clean and obvious.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Launch Screen

The approved launch screen remains unchanged.

All future redesign work starts **after** the launch sequence.
