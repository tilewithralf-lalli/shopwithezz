# 02 - SHOPWITHEZZ BIBLE

## Purpose

This document is the source of truth for the vision, principles, approved experience, and long-term direction of ShopWithEzz.

ShopWithEzz exists to make everyday shopping simpler, quicker, clearer, and less stressful.

The app helps a person prepare a shopping list, manage a budget, collect items in the shop, and use voice controls without being overwhelmed by unnecessary features.

Its guiding promise is:

**Shop beautifully. Spend smarter.**

---

## Who ShopWithEzz Helps

ShopWithEzz is designed for ordinary shoppers of all ages and confidence levels, including people who:

- Want a simple shopping list.
- Need to stay within a budget.
- Prefer large, clear controls and straightforward wording.
- Want hands-free help while shopping.
- Need their information to remain available offline.
- Do not want complicated menus or unnecessary steps.

The app must remain easy enough for a first-time user to understand without training.

---

## The Three Main Jobs

ShopWithEzz must always make these jobs easy:

1. Create and manage a shopping list.
2. Understand spending against a shopping budget.
3. Use the list quickly while moving through a shop.

Every feature must support at least one of these jobs. If it does not make shopping easier, clearer, faster, or more reliable, it does not belong in the main experience.

---

## Approved Core Experience

The approved ShopWithEzz experience includes:

- A clean home screen with the most important information visible.
- A shopping list where items can be added, edited, collected, uncollected, increased, decreased, or removed.
- A shopping budget with clear Budget, Spent, and Left amounts.
- A product scanner for quickly adding items.
- Typed-list and photo-list importing where supported.
- Sharing and printing a shopping list.
- Hands-Free controls available from the Shopping List.
- Spoken list reading for remaining, collected, and complete lists.
- Voice deletion commands with serious confirmation before data is removed.
- Settings for profile, extras, feedback, backup, restore, and app information.
- Local data storage so the shopping list continues to work offline.

The approved loading artwork and the approved home-screen layout must not be redesigned without clear approval.

---

## Hands-Free Principle

Hands-Free is a core ShopWithEzz feature, not a separate novelty.

Voice controls should use natural everyday wording. A shopper should not have to memorise complicated technical phrases.

The Shopping List must provide direct access to the voice controls so the shopper does not need to leave the list and navigate elsewhere.

Spoken responses must be short, clear, useful, and accurate.

Any voice command that deletes information must require confirmation before continuing.

---

## The Granny Test

Every ShopWithEzz screen and feature must pass the Team Lalli61 Granny Test.

Ask:

- Is it obvious what to tap?
- Is the wording easy to understand?
- Is the text readable?
- Is the screen calm rather than crowded?
- Can a first-time user complete the task without help?
- Can the user recover safely from a mistake?

If the answer is no, the feature must be simplified or redesigned before release.

Simple is always better.

---

## Quick, Smart, Fast, and Reliable

The approved quality rule from the project redesign document is:

- Is it quick?
- Is it smart?
- Is it really smart?
- Is it fast?
- Is it reliable?
- Does it pass the Granny Test?

If the answer is no to any one of these questions, review and redesign the affected experience.

---

## Settings Principle

Important controls must live together under Settings instead of being scattered throughout the app.

The standard ShopWithEzz Settings area includes:

- Change Profile
- Extras
- Send Feedback
- Backup
- Restore from Backup
- About and App Version

Share List, Import a List, How to Use, and Hands-Free Commands may be accessed through Extras.

Settings is the place a user goes when they ask, "Where do I change something or fix something?"

---

## Data, Privacy, and Trust

ShopWithEzz must treat the user's information with care.

- Shopping data should remain on the device unless the user deliberately shares or backs it up.
- The app must continue supporting its core list and budget features offline.
- The app must not sell personal information.
- Backup and restore actions must be clear and controlled by the user.
- Destructive actions must never happen silently.
- Pricing, trials, purchases, and limitations must be explained honestly.

Trust comes before downloads.

---

## Backup and Restore Principle

The user must be able to choose where a backup is saved, including a suitable phone folder or connected storage where supported.

A restore must:

1. Let the user choose a ShopWithEzz backup file.
2. Validate the selected file.
3. Ask for confirmation before replacing current app data.
4. Restore the saved ShopWithEzz information accurately.

A final approved source backup must be created before the release build.

---

## Release Principle

ShopWithEzz follows the Team Lalli61 release system:

- A free 31-day beta trial.
- A single one-time purchase.
- No subscriptions.
- Automatic trial lock when the trial expires.
- Permanent unlock after purchase.
- Purchase restoration where supported.

The final Android build must be created locally only after the app, documentation, release system, backups, and real-device testing are complete and approved.

---

## Rules That Must Not Be Broken

1. Listen first and build only what has been requested.
2. Back up every existing file before changing it.
3. Never overwrite or damage a stable approved version.
4. Make one change at a time.
5. Test changes on a real device.
6. Get approval before moving to the next major step.
7. Keep the main experience clean and uncluttered.
8. Do not remove approved features without permission.
9. Do not build the final app package until the project is ready and approved.
10. Keep the project documents accurate and up to date.

---

## Long-Term Vision

ShopWithEzz should become the simplest, most trustworthy everyday shopping assistant for people who want to shop confidently and stay in control of their spending.

Future improvements may make the app smarter, but they must never make it harder to understand.

The app should always feel friendly, practical, professional, and unmistakably Team Lalli61.
