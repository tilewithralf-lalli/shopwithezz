## Team Lalli61 Standard --- Public Download Page

Every Team Lalli61 app must have **one public landing page** before any
download begins.

### Standard Workflow

1.  Share **one public link** everywhere (Facebook, TikTok, website, QR
    codes, etc.).
2.  That link always opens the app's public information page.
3.  The visitor can read about the app, features, privacy, pricing, and
    trial before downloading.
4.  The page automatically detects the visitor's device.

### Device Detection

**Android** - Display the Android download button. - Allow the user to
download immediately.

**iPhone & iPad (iOS)** - Do not display the Android download button. -
Instead, display a professional message:

> **iOS Version Coming Soon**
>
> Thank you for your interest in this application. The current public
> release is available for Android devices only. The iPhone and iPad
> version is being developed and will be released once it has been fully
> tested and meets our quality standards. Thank you for your patience
> and support.
