# ShopWithEzz Bible

## What ShopWithEzz is for

ShopWithEzz makes everyday shopping simpler: create a list, know the budget, collect items in the shop, and get help without clutter.

## The promise

**Shop beautifully. Spend smarter.**

## The core experience

- A clear Home screen with the shopper’s name, budget and list access.
- Shopping lists that can be created, edited, collected, shared, printed, backed up and restored.
- A clear Budget, Spent and Left picture. Budget Bomb warns when a positive budget is exceeded.
- Scanner, photo item import and typed-list import where available.
- Hands-Free controls from the Shopping List.
- Offline local data storage.
- Simple Settings for profile, help, reminders, backup and restore.

## Non-negotiables

- Simple words, readable type and obvious buttons.
- No subscriptions and no hidden charges.
- The live app currently gives a 31-day calendar trial. It warns once a day in the final seven days if reminders are switched on.
- The planned Google Play unlock is a one-time A$35 purchase, only after the app is final.
- Keep personal shopping data on the device unless the shopper deliberately shares or backs it up.

## The Granny Test

Would a first-time shopper know what to tap, understand what will happen, and recover safely from a mistake? If not, simplify it.
