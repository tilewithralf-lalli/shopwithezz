## 4. COMPARE MY LIST — LOCKED PLAN (NOT YET IMPLEMENTED)

### Purpose

Compare My List is an optional future upgrade. It must work from the shopper's normal ShopWithEzz list, including lists without prices. The shopper taps **COMPARE MY LIST**, and ShopWithEzz gives one simple whole-basket decision:

- **STAY & SHOP**
- **BETTER STORE — SAVE $XX.XX**
- **SENSIBLE SPLIT**

The first supported shopping area is postcode **4223**. Core supermarkets are **Woolworths, Coles and ALDI**. Big W, Chemist Warehouse, Priceline and Kmart can be optional later where useful price data is available.

### Location choices

Keep the location step simple:

- **Near Me** — use the phone's location only with permission. AI must never guess the shopper's physical location.
- **My Usual Shopping Area** — let the shopper save their normal area, for example **The Pines**.
- **Choose Another Location** — let the shopper type/select a different area, for example **Robina Town Centre**.

Only compare stores and shopping areas supported by reliable store and price data.

### Locked Two-Brains design

- **AI understanding brain:** understands what the shopper means, separates exact-brand requests from generic items, handles pack sizes and quantities, and identifies sensible equivalents.
- **ShopWithEzz comparison brain:** owns the list, location, budget, verified prices, unit-value calculations, totals, savings and final recommendation.
- The locked summary is: **AI understanding + ShopWithEzz price engine = one ridiculously simple shopping answer.**

### Price and matching rules

- An exact-brand request stays exact. Example: Nescafé Gold must not silently become a different coffee.
- Generic items may use a sensible cheaper or better-value equivalent.
- Compare unit value where pack sizes differ.
- Each usable match must include the retailer/source, product, brand where relevant, pack size, current price, exact-versus-alternative status, unit/value data and verification status.
- AI-generated or guessed prices are never trusted by themselves.
- ShopWithEzz must never invent a current price.
- If a price cannot be verified, show **PRICE UNAVAILABLE / UNVERIFIED** and exclude it from the savings calculation.
- Show the current-list total where known, the best verified alternative total and the potential savings.
- Comparison modes may include exact brand, specials versus normal price and sensible equivalents.
- Travel, budget thresholds and split-shopping logic come after the basic whole-basket comparison is proven.
- The real question is not **where is every item cheapest?** It is **what is the smartest practical way to buy my shopping list?**
- Do not send shoppers across town or to several stores for a tiny saving.
- A two-store split is most useful when both stores are in the same shopping centre or the saving genuinely justifies the extra stop.
- The shopper always makes the final choice.

### Search/data decision

- Keep this separate from the working released app until the comparison route is proven.
- **WhichGrocer was investigated and rejected as the final dependency.**
- **Firecrawl was also rejected for the final in-app price route.**
- The preferred direction is **Route B: on-demand public retailer search**, keeping running costs low and avoiding a paid per-search API where possible.
- Crawlee, Playwright and SearXNG were considered as possible supporting technologies.
- Gemini/Tavily/Brave experiments are research options only; they are not locked production dependencies.
- Any method must be tested for retailer coverage, current-price reliability, source quality, speed, integration difficulty and predictable cost before being connected to ShopWithEzz.

### Perplexity investigation — final decision 12 September 2026

- Perplexity was investigated specifically for the future **Compare My List** upgrade, not as a general app for Team LALLI61.
- It is not available as a normal Perplexity plugin connection for this Codex setup.
- Technically, Perplexity Search/Sonar could be connected to a ShopWithEzz backend and can return live web results, structured search data and citations.
- However, production API searches are pay-as-you-go. Creating an API key is not the same as having free ongoing production searches.
- A recurring per-search cost does not suit ShopWithEzz's **A$34.99 one-time-purchase** model because Team LALLI61 would continue paying whenever customers compare lists.
- Therefore **Perplexity is rejected for the production Compare My List system unless it later provides a genuinely free production API suitable for the required usage**.

### Locked cost rule

- No paid search API.
- No compulsory subscription.
- No credit-purchase system.
- No recurring charge each time a shopper taps **COMPARE MY LIST**.
- Continue pursuing **Route B: an on-demand public-retailer search controlled by ShopWithEzz**, using free/self-hosted technology such as Playwright, Crawlee or SearXNG where technically and legally suitable.
- Gemini may be tested as the understanding brain only if its usable production allowance is genuinely free, sufficient and controllable. A temporary trial or introductory credit does not qualify.
- Do not lock any provider into production until a real Australian supermarket test proves current prices, product/pack-size matching, sources, reliability and sustainable zero/acceptable ongoing cost.
